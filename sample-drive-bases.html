Round does not work with 4WD. A round body has to enclose all four wheels and the motors at both corners, so it ends up wider than the 225 mm limit. Use a square, rectangle, or U-shape for 4WD.

What the drawing is based on

  • Motor: Pololu #4805, 47:1 25D×67L mm HP 6V with 48 CPR encoder; 12.5 mm output shaft (Pololu product page).
  • Wheel diameters 60, 70, 80, and 90 mm are the Pololu wheel sizes that fit the 4 mm hub (Pololu). 70 mm is the Components reference pick; the others change speed and ground clearance, so rerun the Drive Chain Calculator.
  • Wheel and track widths (≈8, 15, 25, 20 mm) are example sizes, not chosen parts. Ball caster 19 mm (#955).
  • Back to back: both motors on one centerline. Staggered: one motor shifted forward, one back, so they overlap side to side and the body can be narrower. The two wheels then sit about 27 mm out of line with each other.
  • Axle position applies to 2WD with wheels. The Session 02 ramp test slipped with front drive because weight shifted off the drive wheels.
  • 4WD wheelbase is the distance between front and rear axles. Shorter turns more easily; longer is steadier on ramps.
  • Spin circle: the area the robot sweeps turning in place, compared to one 30 × 30 cm tile (rule 3.1.2). The center is midway between the drive wheels for 2WD and the middle of the wheelbase for 4WD and tracks.
  • The 3 mm motor-to-wheel gap and other clearances are placeholders; real values depend on hubs and brackets.
  • The 225 mm limit is the class size target (25 cm tunnel minus 10%). Length has no limit.
