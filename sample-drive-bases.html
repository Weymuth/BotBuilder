What the drawings are based on

  • Motor: Pololu #4805, 47:1 25D×67L mm HP 6V with 48 CPR encoder; 12.5 mm output shaft (Pololu product page).
  • Wheel diameters 60, 70, 80, and 90 mm are the Pololu wheel sizes that fit the 4 mm hub. 70 mm is the Components reference pick; the others change speed and ground clearance, so rerun the Drive Chain Calculator.
  • Wheel and track widths (≈8, 15, 25, 20 mm) are example sizes, not chosen parts.
  • Back to back: both motors on one centerline. Staggered: one motor shifted forward, one back, so the body can be narrower. The two wheels then sit out of line: about 27 mm with the Pololu bracket, about 33 mm with a printed cradle.
  • Axle position applies to 2WD with wheels. The Session 02 ramp test slipped with front drive because weight shifted off the drive wheels.
  • Spin circle: the area swept turning in place, compared to one 30 × 30 cm tile (rule 3.1.2).
  • Ball casters (Pololu dimension drawings): ⅜″ #950/951 is 10.1 mm tall, ½″ #952/953 is 13.5 mm, ¾″ metal #955 is 20.9 mm, measured from the mounting face to the bottom of the ball. The ¾″ comes with 1/16″ and 1/8″ spacers, giving about 22.5, 24.1, or 25.7 mm. The smaller casters also ship with spacers, but Pololu does not list their sizes, so they are not offered here.
  • Pololu bracket #2676 (Pololu dimension diagram): 2 mm aluminum, foot 52 × 21.7 mm, upright 27 mm, shaft center 14.5 mm from the mounting face. It bolts to the top or bottom of the deck only. A shim under the foot moves the motor away from the deck.
  • 3D-printed cradle: ≈3 mm wall is a placeholder; students design their own. It can sit on top, underneath, or through a slot in the deck at any depth.
  • Level (2WD): the deck underside has to sit at caster height at the casters and at the drive axle. The page compares the two and shows the gap.
  • Ramp angles are geometry estimates from the drawing, measured from the wheel and caster contact points to the deck edge or the lowest part of the belly (conservative). Ramps can be up to 25° (rule 3.7). Approach is the front, departure is the rear, and break-over is the belly between contact points.
  • QTRX-MD-08A, Pololu #4448: 61.0 × 20.0 × 3.0 mm, 8 sensors at 8 mm pitch; best sensing distance 10 mm, high-reflectance objects detectable to about 40 mm (Pololu product page). The black line is 1 to 2 cm wide (rule 3.3.1). Below 10 mm the board sits under the speed-bump height, so students must choose a height and a way past bumps.
  • OpenMV Cam RT1062: 45 × 36 × 29 mm, 20 g; with its OV5640 sensor the field of view is 65.9° horizontal and 51.8° vertical (OpenMV product page). The drawing assumes the 45 mm side stands upright and the 29 mm side is the lens depth. Floor view is a pinhole-camera estimate.
  • VL53L8CX carrier, Pololu #3419: 13 × 23 × 3 mm, 45° horizontal and vertical field of view, 20 mm to 4 m (Pololu product page). Drawn centered on the front edge, facing forward, 23 mm side across.
  • VL53L4CD: 18° field of view (ST). The board is not chosen yet; it is drawn at the size of Pololu carrier #3692 (12.7 × 17.8 × 2.2 mm) as an example. Side spots are drawn 30 mm behind the front edge.
  • Step 3 checks are footprint checks from above plus the heights shown. Wires, headers, and sensor mounts are not drawn.
  • Speed bumps are up to 1 cm high (rule 3.5.1). The 225 mm limit is the class size target (25 cm tunnel minus 10%). Length has no limit.
